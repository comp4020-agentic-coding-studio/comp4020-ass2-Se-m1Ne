The capstone: an original picture-book work in which rule-breaking supports
the story rather than decorating it. Resolved does not mean polished — a
photographed paper prototype can be a finished answer to this brief if it
reads as a complete, coherent experience.

## The brief

> Make a picture book that deliberately breaks one or more of its own rules,
> and revise it after watching someone read it.

Format is open — physical book, digital work, photographed prototype,
collage, illustrated prototype, or an unusual structure are all acceptable.
Combining more than one form of misbehaviour is allowed, but only where the
combination earns its place; adding techniques to lengthen a checklist counts
against the work, not for it.

## What you submit

The finished work, in whatever format you have made it, plus a Reader Note of
400 words maximum explaining the intended reader experience, what happened
during testing, what changed, and why the final work breaks the rules it
breaks.
