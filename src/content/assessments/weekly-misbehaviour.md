This is a running practice, not a single deliverable. Each teaching week sets
one small, rough experiment under the heading **This Week's Misbehaviour** —
something makeable in roughly 15–30 minutes with whatever materials are on
hand. Together the twelve prompts build a personal Misbehaviour Toolbox that
the three larger assessments draw on directly.

## The brief

> Try the week's prompt, and keep what you made — even the ones that fail.

There is no quality bar here beyond a clear intention. A prompt that does not
work is still useful evidence, as long as you can say what it was testing and
why it did not land.

## What you submit

Whatever the week's prompt produces — a photo, a scan, a short note, a
rearranged sequence — kept somewhere you can point to at review. Completing 10
of the 12 prompts is enough for the full mark; there is no separate rubric for
quality.
